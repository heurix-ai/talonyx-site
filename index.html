What this is

Beacon is a call routing and data platform. It is not an insurance company, a carrier, or an agency, and it does not sell insurance or give insurance advice. It connects your account to call exchanges and routes calls to your phone in real time.

How it works

  1. Request access and tell us your license and state.
  2. Set your price tier, cheaper and slower, or pricier and faster.
  3. Calls route to your phone as they come in. You see them land in your dashboard.
  4. Your account is charged based on actual call connections, not promises.

What matters

No guaranteed sales outcomes. No refunds for calls that connect but don't close. Pricing is transparent before you commit, and every call you're charged for shows up in your own call log.
