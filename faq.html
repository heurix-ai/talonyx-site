Beacon by Talonyx  ·  call marketplace portal for licensed agents

What is Beacon?

Beacon by Talonyx is a call marketplace portal for licensed insurance agents. It routes qualified inbound calls to your line and runs on a prepaid wallet. Beacon is a call routing and data platform. It is not an insurance company, carrier, or agency, and it does not sell insurance.

How does pricing work?

You pay per connected call from a prepaid wallet. Your price is shown in your account before you commit, and every charge appears in your call log. You are charged on call connection, not on whether a call closes.

What is founder pricing?

Members who sign up before launch lock in early founder pricing on their account. Founder spots are limited.

How do I sign up?

Use the signup form on the Talonyx site. You provide your name, business, state, and license number. Applications are reviewed before access is granted.

How do I get support?

Email support@talonyx.com any time, or call the Talonyx frontdesk line. Live phone support is for members, so have your member code ready. Anyone can leave a request on the line and the team will follow up.

Is this insurance?

No. Beacon is a call routing and data platform. You remain responsible for your own compliance when handling calls. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.
