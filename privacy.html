Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026  ·  Talonyx, operated by Heurix LLC

Who we are

Beacon is a product of Talonyx LLC. Beacon is a call routing and data platform. It is not an insurance company, carrier, or agency, it does not sell insurance, and it does not provide insurance or legal advice.

Our role under privacy law

For the personal information processed on behalf of our customers (licensed agents), Beacon acts as a service provider, not the owner of that data. We process it only to deliver the service and under the terms of our customer agreement, consistent with the CCPA and comparable state laws.

What we collect

  • Account information you provide: name, business name, email, state, and license number.
  • Billing information processed by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Call metadata routed through the platform: call identifier, timestamp, duration, connection status, and destination region.

What we do not collect

Beacon does not record, store, or listen to the audio of calls routed to you. The platform sees call metadata only, never the live call content.

How we use information

  • To route calls, operate your wallet, and show your call log and balance.
  • To verify eligibility and licensing at signup.
  • To detect fraud, enforce acceptable use, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example, payment processing and call routing infrastructure), under contract, and only as needed. We do not sell personal information.

SMS messaging

If you opt in, Talonyx sends account and service text messages (such as login links, call and wallet notifications, and support replies) to the mobile number you provide. Message and data rates may apply and message frequency varies. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your state, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to appeal a decision. Contact us at support@talonyx.com to make a request.
